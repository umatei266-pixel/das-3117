# Săptămâna Spațiului — design
Cinematic Romanian documentary presentation. Ten 16:9 chapters; brief on-screen summaries and fully translated source prose in a reader drawer.
Palette: cosmic black #05080d, warm white #f4f3ef, blue-grey #929dac, ice cyan #91dbe9. Real NASA/ESA imagery, local assets, slow photographic zooms, subtle stars and orbital lines.
Typography: Manrope display and body, IBM Plex Mono metadata. Light oversized editorial headings, tracked uppercase labels, generous negative space.
Layout: 16:9 stage, left titles, right photographic subjects, understated identity and chapter labels. Player bar, segmented timeline, chapter overview and full-text drawer.
Motion: Motion crossfades and staggered text. CSS star drift, reduced-motion support. Autoplay 18 seconds per scene; overlays pause playback.
Frontend only: page composition, presentation component, content module, global CSS, public images. No new API, database or accounts.

## Revision — user correction
All translated source paragraphs must be visible directly on their respective slide, not exclusively in a drawer. Replace oversized minimal copy with compact editorial title and a two-column paragraph layout, legible against a stronger dark photographic scrim. Keep 16:9 and real images. Use unmistakable directional slide/blur transitions with a cinematic chapter interstitial and light sweep. Autoplay allows 45 seconds per slide for reading.

## Hyperspace transition revision
Replace simple chapter fade with a 2.8-second cinematic hyperspace jump: radial star trails, concentric expanding portal rings, a preview of the next scene through the portal, cyan/amber lens flare and floating chapter title. No rapid flashing. Effects are pointer-transparent and reduced-motion disables the tunnel. Text and 45-second playback remain unchanged.

## Distinct scene transitions — supersedes repeated hyperspace
Each destination has its own 7-second transition with separate composition and motion: Earth orbital departure, solar-system flythrough, lunar eclipse, Martian dust curtain, opening spacecraft airlock, ISS orbital tracking, rocket ascent, Webb mirror unfolding, nebula bloom, and Earth-return iris. Scene text stays unchanged. Replace repeated zoom/blur with a neutral fade underneath. Explicit slow pacing with a visible transition-name/progress indicator. Honor reduced motion.
